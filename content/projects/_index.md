The site is backed by several engineering projects. Each is documented in its
own repository and surfaced here.

## Decentralized Governance (DAO)

Solidity / Hardhat smart contracts implementing commit-reveal voting and
on-chain proposal lifecycles. Source: `contracts/dao/` in the site repo.

## Knowledge Graph pipeline

A build-time generator that turns published posts, tags, repositories and gists
into a navigable semantic graph (see [Knowledge Graph](/knowledge-graph/)).
Rendered with D3 from `assets/js/knowledge-graph.js`.

## Research substrate

Experimental notes and gists under `content/gists/` and the `src/` working
directory — optimization, automation, and decentralized-systems experiments.
